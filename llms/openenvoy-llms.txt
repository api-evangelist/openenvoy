# OpenEnvoy: Autonomous Finance for Complex Spend


> OpenEnvoy is an AI-native financial operations platform that helps enterprise and mid-market finance teams automate accounts payable, accounts receivable, and invoice processing end-to-end. Founded with technology developed for leaders like NVIDIA, Qualcomm, and Goldman Sachs, OpenEnvoy has prevented over $1.16B in overbillings and achieves 99.8% accuracy in transaction verification. Named Gartner Cool Vendor in Sourcing & Procurement.


Key terms: AP Automation, Accounts Payable Automation, Accounts Receivable Automation, Invoice Processing, Invoice Automation, Three-Way Matching, Touchless AP, E-Invoicing, Cash Application, Order Entry Automation, Working Capital, Spend Visibility, Fraud Detection, Duplicate Payment Prevention, ERP Integration, GL Coding, Supplier Overbilling, Finance Automation, Autonomous Finance


## OpenEnvoy Platform Capabilities


- [Platform Overview](https://www.openenvoy.com/openenvoy-platform): OpenEnvoy's AI-native platform digitizes clerical work across finance operations, from invoice capture to payment, with no supplier onboarding required.
- [Capture and Extract](https://www.openenvoy.com/openenvoy-platform): Ingest 100% of invoices from any format email, EDI, scanned PDF, or supplier portals with AI-powered data extraction and automatic GL coding. No OCR templates or manual uploads required.
- [Contracts and Reconciliation](https://www.openenvoy.com/openenvoy-platform): Match invoices against POs, contracts, rate sheets, and receipts with high accuracy. Covers simple purchases through complex direct spend and freight transactions.
- [Intelligent Workflow](https://www.openenvoy.com/openenvoy-platform): AI-powered approval routing that learns from your team's behavior, auto-suggests GL codes, and resolves exceptions automatically. Multi-level approvals with no-code logic.
- [Deployment and Integrations](https://www.openenvoy.com/openenvoy-platform): Go live in under 60 days. Plug-and-play integration with SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, QuickBooks, and other ERPs no IT projects or custom code required.
- [Order Entry Automation](https://www.openenvoy.com/order-entry): Automatically extract and validate order information, sync with your ERP in real time, and eliminate manual order processing errors.
- [Working Capital](https://www.openenvoy.com/openenvoy-platform): Optimize payment timing by matching invoices with contracted payment terms to stretch DPO or capture early-payment discounts.


## OpenEnvoy Products


- [Accounts Payable Software](https://www.openenvoy.com/accounts-payable-software): AI-first, zero-touch AP automation with touchless invoice processing from capture to payment. Eliminates losses to fraud, duplicates, and supplier overbilling with real-time pre-audit on every invoice.
- [Accounts Receivable](https://www.openenvoy.com/accounts-receivable): Automate customer billing reconciliation to eliminate disputes, reduce DSO, and prevent revenue leakage from under-billing, even with complex contracts, payment terms, and service charges.
- [E-Invoicing](https://www.openenvoy.com/capabilities/electronic-invoice-system): AI-powered electronic invoicing system that captures, validates, and routes invoices across formats, supports multi-currency and global compliance standards, and integrates with all major ERPs.
- [Cash Application](https://www.openenvoy.com/openenvoy-platform): Automate cash application, order entry, and reconciliation to improve accuracy and free your team for strategic work.
- [Automated Invoice Processing](https://www.openenvoy.com/capabilities/automated-invoice-processing-software): End-to-end invoice workflow automation that eliminates manual data entry, prevents duplicate payments, and reduces processing time from 10+ days to under 1 hour.
- [Invoice Workflow Software](https://www.openenvoy.com/capabilities/invoice-workflow-software): No-code approval routing based on value, GL account, or department. Each step is timestamped and accessible in a centralized dashboard. Learns from past behavior to resolve exceptions 2x faster.
- [Visibility and Control](https://www.openenvoy.com/visibility-and-control): Real-time dashboards for invoice status, approval delays, spend analytics, and cash flow tracking. Full audit readiness across all transactions.
